Employer-Sponsered Insurance
  Source: https://www.kff.org/health-costs/health-policy-101-employer-sponsored-health-insurance/ 
  Information:
  - The largest source of health coverage for U.S. residents under age 65.
  - ESI often results in uneven coverage, especially for those with low wages or those working at smaller firms
    What is ESI?
    - Group Health Insurance: under a policy or plan offered by a sponsoring group, such as an employer, union or trade association
    - an individual is sponsored specifically by an employer (or sometimes jointly by one or more employers and a union or by a group of employers)
    - An employer providing health benefits for workers and their families (“plan enrollees”) can fund them in one of two ways
         - Insured Plan: Employers may purchase a health insurance policy from a state-licensed health insurer
         - Self-funded Plan: The employer can pay for health care for the plan enrollees directly with its own assets
